Condition

Heel pain treatment in Santa Rosa, CA.

Heel pain has many possible causes — plantar fasciitis most commonly, but also heel spurs, Achilles issues, fat pad atrophy, and more. Dr. Lund's approach starts with a precise diagnosis so the treatment actually works.

Symptoms

Heel pain comes in different flavors.

  • !
    Sharp pain under the heel, especially in the morning.
  • !
    Pain at the back of the heel, sometimes worse after running.
  • !
    Deep, bruised feeling in the heel pad.
  • !
    Tenderness along the Achilles tendon.
  • !
    Pain that's persisted more than 3 weeks despite home treatment.
Dr. Lund's approach

First the diagnosis. Then the plan.

Step 01

Pinpoint the cause

Hands-on exam, imaging when indicated, and a careful history to identify which structure is generating pain.

Step 02

Treat what's actually broken

Conservative methods targeted to the diagnosis — orthotics, shockwave, physical therapy, or targeted injections.

Step 03

Prevent recurrence

Footwear guidance, a home routine, and structured follow-up to keep the pain from returning.
