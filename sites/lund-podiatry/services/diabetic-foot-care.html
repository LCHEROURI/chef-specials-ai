Diabetic foot care in Santa Rosa, CA.

Diabetes changes how your feet feel, heal, and age. Dr. Lund's diabetic program is built around preventing problems before they start — with structured screenings, education, and a personalized plan that grows with you.

Why this matters

Prevention is everything.

Most diabetic foot complications are preventable with consistent care. The risk isn't the diagnosis — it's the surprises. Dr. Lund's program is designed to remove surprises.

  • !
    Numbness, tingling, or burning in your feet.
  • !
    Slow-healing cuts, blisters, or sores.
  • !
    Dry, cracked skin on heels and toes that won't resolve.
  • !
    Changes in foot shape (especially the arch).
  • !
    A history of ulcers or infection.
