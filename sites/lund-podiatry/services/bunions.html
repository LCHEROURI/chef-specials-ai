Condition

Bunion treatment in Santa Rosa, CA.

A bunion is more than a bump — it's a structural alignment issue that gradually worsens. Dr. Lund's approach combines custom orthotics, footwear coaching, and modern minimally invasive surgical techniques when conservative care isn't enough.

Symptoms

Are you seeing (and feeling) these signs?

  • !
    Painful bump at the base of your big toe.
  • !
    Big toe drifting toward the second toe.
  • !
    Redness, swelling, or burning around the joint.
  • !
    Difficulty finding comfortable shoes — even wide widths feel tight.
  • !
    Pain after long periods of standing or walking.
  • !
    You've tried padding, wider shoes, or inserts without lasting relief.
Dr. Lund's approach

A plan that respects your timeline.

Step 01

Map the alignment

Imaging, gait analysis, and a structural exam to understand how your bunion developed.

Step 02

Conservative first

Custom orthotics, footwear coaching, and joint-strengthening routines that often delay or eliminate the need for surgery.

Step 03

Modern surgical options

When surgery is the right call, minimally invasive techniques mean smaller scars, less downtime, and a faster return to life.
