Ingrown toenail treatment in Santa Rosa, CA.

Symptoms

These are signs you should come in.

  • !
    Throbbing pain along one or both sides of the toenail.
  • !
    Redness or swelling at the nail edge.
  • !
    Drainage or pus (sign of infection).
  • !
    Tenderness when wearing shoes or even a sock.
  • !
    Recurring ingrown nails despite careful trimming.
Dr. Lund's approach

One visit. Real relief.

Step 01

Quick assessment

A focused exam to identify the offending border and any infection.

Step 02

Gentle in-office procedure

A local anesthetic, careful removal of the ingrown border, and treatment of any infection present.

Step 03

Prevent recurrence

If nails keep regrowing inward, a minor matrixectomy stops the problematic border from coming back.
