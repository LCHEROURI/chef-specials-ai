Condition

Hammertoe treatment in Santa Rosa, CA.

Toes that bend or curl — slowly getting worse, rubbing on shoes, forming painful corns. Dr. Lund's hammertoe plans are built around how flexible the toe still is. The right approach depends on that.

Symptoms

Are your toes doing this?

  • !
    One or more toes bend or curl at the middle joint.
  • !
    Pain at the top of the bent toe from shoe friction.
  • !
    Corns or calluses on the toe tip or top knuckle.
  • !
    Difficulty wearing closed shoes, even with extra room.
  • !
    The toe won't straighten on its own anymore.
Dr. Lund's approach

Flexible vs. rigid — different plans.

Step 01

Assess flexibility

A focused exam to determine whether the toe is still flexible (correctable with conservative care) or rigid (likely needs surgical alignment).

Step 02

Conservative when possible

For flexible hammertoes: pads, taping, custom orthotics, and targeted exercises to keep the toe mobile and prevent progression.

Step 03

Surgical correction when needed

For rigid hammertoes: a small procedure to release tight tendons and realign the joint. Most patients walk in a protective shoe within days.
