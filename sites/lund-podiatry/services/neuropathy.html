Neuropathy treatment in Santa Rosa, CA.

Burning, tingling, numbness in your feet — and the fear of what could go wrong because you can't feel it. Dr. Lund's neuropathy program focuses on managing symptoms, slowing progression, and protecting your long-term foot health.

Symptoms

Living with these sensations?

  • !
    Burning or hot sensations in the feet, especially at night.
  • !
    Tingling, 'pins and needles,' or buzzing.
  • !
    Numbness — feet feel 'asleep' even when you're standing.
  • !
    Sharp or shooting pains that come and go.
  • !
    Difficulty with balance or feeling unsteady on your feet.
Dr. Lund's approach

Manage, protect, progress.

Step 01

Identify the cause

A focused exam and nerve-function testing to understand what's driving your symptoms.

Step 02

Manage symptoms

Targeted treatment plans — medications when indicated, balance work, and protective footwear.

Step 03

Prevent complications

Recurring visits, structured self-checks, and a long-term plan that adapts as your nerves change.
