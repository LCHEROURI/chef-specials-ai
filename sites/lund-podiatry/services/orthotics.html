Custom orthotics — precision support for the way you walk.

A custom orthotic is a prescription insert fabricated to your exact foot shape, gait, and goals. It's the simplest upgrade most of our patients make — and the foundation that lets every other treatment work better.

Why custom

Not a one-size-fits-all insert.

Store-bought inserts cushion. Custom orthotics correct. The difference shows up not in comfort, but in how your foot, ankle, knee, and back load over a long walk, run, or workday.

Built for your gait

Digital gait analysis identifies exactly how you pronate, supinate, and load the foot through every step.

Built for your shape

A 3D scan or foam impression captures your exact arch, heel position, and forefoot width.

Prescribed, not guessed

Dr. Lund prescribes the right type — functional for alignment, accommodative for pressure relief.

Conditions orthotics help with

A foundation across most plans of care.

Custom orthotics aren't just a single-condition treatment — they're the foundation that makes many other treatments work better. Dr. Lund prescribes them as part of personalized plans for:

  • ✓
    Plantar fasciitis — corrects the loading pattern that overloads the fascia.
  • ✓
    Bunions & hammertoes — slows progression and supports post-surgical alignment.
  • ✓
    Heel pain — redistributes shock and pressure across the heel.
  • ✓
    Diabetic foot care — accommodative orthotics protect high-pressure areas from ulcers.
  • ✓
    Flat feet / high arches — provides the support your foot naturally can't.
  • ✓
    Sports & running injuries — addresses structural causes of recurring overuse.
  • ✓
    General standing-fatigue — long workdays on hard floors become genuinely easier.
Dr. Lund's process

From exam to your shoes — four clear steps.

Step 01

Exam + gait analysis

Dr. Lund assesses alignment, range of motion, and how you actually walk — not just how your foot looks.

Step 02

3D scan or foam cast

A precise capture of your foot shape — comfortable, fast, and accurate.

Step 03

Lab fabrication

Your prescription is sent to a podiatric lab. The custom orthotic is fabricated to your exact specifications.

Step 04

Fitting + break-in coaching

A short follow-up to confirm fit. Dr. Lund gives you a written break-in schedule and checks back in.

Why Dr. Lund

Prescribed. Not sold.

Most orthotic failures come from prescriptions that don't match the patient's actual problem. Dr. Lund only prescribes orthotics when clinical assessment shows they'll help — and chooses functional or accommodative based on the diagnosis, not the latest catalog.
