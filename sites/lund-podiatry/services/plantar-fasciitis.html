Symptoms

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • !
    Sharp pain with your first steps in the morning — usually settles within minutes but returns after sitting.
  • !
    Stabbing pain at the bottom of the heel — especially after long periods of standing or exercise.
  • !
    Stiffness or burning along the arch of your foot.
  • !
    Pain that has lasted more than a few weeks and isn't responding to stretching or store inserts.
  • !
    One foot is worse than the other — or it has started affecting how you walk.
  • !
    You've tried resting, icing, or over-the-counter inserts without lasting relief.
Dr. Lund's approach

Three steps to walking pain-free.

Step 01

Diagnose precisely

Gait analysis, on-site imaging, and a hands-on exam to find the exact cause — not just the symptom.

Step 02

Treat conservatively

Custom orthotics, shockwave therapy, targeted physical therapy, and a stretching routine for your daily life.

Step 03

Surgery only if needed

Surgery is reserved for the rare, chronic case where conservative care has been exhausted. Most patients never need it.
