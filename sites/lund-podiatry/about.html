Philosophy

Simple does not mean short.

Many foot and ankle problems respond to straightforward, evidence-based care. Dr. Lund's approach begins with the simplest interventions that have the strongest evidence — and escalates only when those aren't enough.

Listen first

Every visit starts with a thoughtful conversation about your foot, your goals, and what you've already tried.

Diagnose precisely

On-site imaging and gait assessment. No rushed guesses — a real understanding of why it hurts.

Treat personally

A plan written for you — not for the average patient. You leave with a written roadmap.
