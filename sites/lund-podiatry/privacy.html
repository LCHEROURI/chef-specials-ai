Privacy & data handling

Plain-English summary of how this site handles the information you share with us. Formal notice: this page is a draft for a prototype deployment and must be reviewed by counsel before going live.

What we collect when you use the site

By default — nothing. This site does not set tracking cookies, does not run third-party analytics, and does not fingerprint your device. Pages render with no JavaScript-exfiltrated telemetry. Your read-only browsing does not give us any personal information.

What we collect when you submit a form

The contact form on /contact.html collects these fields when you choose to submit them:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Condition treated (e.g. "heel pain")
  • How long you've had symptoms
  • Insurance plan (or "self-pay")
  • Best time to reach you

These fields are required because Dr. Lund's office triages the request by hand — calling or emailing a real person, not running an autoresponder.

How we transmit and store that form submission

  1. In transit: TLS 1.2+ at the Vercel edge.
  2. Bot protection: Cloudflare Turnstile verifies a non-interactive token before the form submits. The verification request includes your IP and the token — never your name, email, or description.
  3. Rate limit: 10 submissions / IP / minute. Excess traffic is rejected without storage.
  4. Relay: the serverless function forwards the lead to one of two email-relay providers, in this priority order:
    • Resend (used when a signed HIPAA BAA is in place). Highest priority.
    • Web3Forms (fallback). Used only when Resend is unset or its BAA is unsigned.
  5. Logs: function logs record condition, duration, insurance — never name, phone, or email. Logs are PHI-light so a casual log-reader can't reconstruct a patient.

What we do with the information

Dr. Lund's staff uses the lead to call or email you back during the contact window you selected. We do not sell, share, or rent contact information to any third party under any circumstance. We do not use the information for marketing automation sequences, lead-scoring algorithms, or AI training.

What we do not collect

  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, Plausible, etc.).
  • No session recordings or behavioral heatmaps.
  • No cross-site cookies, no fingerprinting, no advertising IDs.
  • No social-login or OAuth flow that bounces you through Google or Facebook.

Your controls

  • Ask us to delete a lead: call (303) 555-0123 or reply "delete" to the email you received. We will remove the message from the email-relay account within 5 business days.
  • Ask us what we have: same channels; we will tell you exactly what was relayed.
  • Browser controls: the site honors the standard browser DNT (Do Not Track) signal as a matter of practice, although we ship no third-party trackers that would respect it either way.

Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Submitting on behalf of a minor is fine — please note that in the "anything else" field of the form.
